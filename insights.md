 1:Customer Segment Spending

VIP customers have the highest average customer spending at KSh 253,251.88, 
followed by Corporate customers at KSh 197,958.71, 
while Retail customers have the lowest average spending at KSh 163,575.21. 
VIP customers therefore spend approximately 54.8% more per customer than Retail customers,
 while Corporate customers spend about 21.0% more than Retail customers.

Why it matters: 

The results show that customer value differs significantly across the three segments.
 VIP and Corporate customers generate higher spending per customer, 
 making them particularly important for revenue retention and relationship management.

Recommendation: 

Duka Smart should prioritize retention of VIP and Corporate customers through personalized promotions,
 loyalty benefits and proactive customer engagement. 
 The business should also identify high-spending Retail customers and use cross-selling and 
 upselling opportunities to increase their average spending.
 
  2:Product Category Revenue

Finding: 

Mobile Phones generated the highest completed-order revenue at KSh 6,942,060.65,
closely followed by Computing at KSh 6,596,221.70 and Electronics at KSh 5,515,359.20. 
Together, these three categories generated approximately 78.8% of total completed-order revenue of KSh 24,155,371.70.
 Accessories generated the lowest revenue at only KSh 1,077,496.10, representing approximately 4.5% of total revenue.

Why it matters:

Duka Smart's revenue is concentrated in Mobile Phones,Computing and Electronics. 
Strong performance in these categories is therefore important to overall business revenue. 
However, high revenue does not automatically mean high profitability because product costs and discounts may differ between categories.

Recommendation: 

Management should maintain sufficient inventory and monitor pricing closely for Mobile Phones, Computing and Electronics 
because they are the main revenue drivers. 
At the same time, the business should investigate the low contribution from Accessories and consider bundling accessories with phones
 and other electronics to increase cross-selling. 
 Before increasing investment in any category, management should compare these revenue results with category-level profit margins.
 
 3: Most Profitable Product Category

Finding: 

Mobile Phones are the most profitable product category, generating KSh 728,760.65 in profit from completed orders.
 Computing is a close second at KSh 700,021.70, while Electronics generated KSh 547,359.20. 
 Home Appliances and Accessories generated KSh 458,034.05 and KSh 456,746.10, respectively.

Why it matters:

 Mobile Phones are the strongest category in both revenue and absolute profit, 
 confirming their importance to Duka Smart's financial performance. However,Computing is only KSh 28,738.95 behind Mobile Phones in profit, 
 so management should not overlook it.
 Accessories are also noteworthy because, despite generating the lowest revenue,they produced almost the same total profit as Home Appliances.

Recommendation:

Duka Smart should protect inventory availability and carefully manage pricing and discounts for Mobile Phones and
 Computing because they are the two strongest profit generators.Management should also investigate the profit margin of Accessories.
 If Accessories have a higher margin than the other categories, the company could increase cross-selling by bundling accessories with mobile phones
  and other electronics.
  
4: Insight: Revenue versus Profitability by Product Category 

Finding:

Mobile Phones generated the highest revenue and absolute profit among all product categories. 
However, their profit margin was relatively low at approximately 10.50%. Computing followed with a margin of 10.61%, 
Electronics recorded 9.92%, and Home Appliances achieved 11.38%. Accessories had the highest profit margin at approximately 42.39%, 
despite generating the lowest revenue.

Why it matters:

The results reveal that high revenue does not necessarily translate into high profitability. 
Mobile Phones are important revenue drivers, but Accessories generate a substantially higher return on every shilling of revenue. 
This suggests that Accessories have a more favorable relationship between selling price and product cost. 
Focusing exclusively on high-revenue categories could cause management to overlook products with stronger profit margins.

Recommendation:

Duka Smart Electronics should maintain its investment in Mobile Phones because of their contribution to revenue and 
total profit while investigating opportunities to improve their margins through supplier negotiations and discount control.
 Management should also consider expanding Accessories sales through product bundling, especially with mobile phones and laptops. 
 Electronics should receive a pricing and cost review to identify opportunities to improve its relatively low profit margin.

5: Why Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth Matters

Short-Term Momentum Tracking: It reveals whether the business is expanding or contracting month-to-month, 
acting as an early warning system for sales slowdowns.

Recommendations

Investigate Extreme Volatility: Once chronological, look for months with sudden massive spikes or sharp drops.
Spikes help to determine if they were driven by specific marketing promotions, bulk enterprise orders, 
or seasonal shopping events (like Black Friday/End-of-Year sales), while dips aid in the check if low revenue months were caused by stockouts, supply chain delays, or a lack of promotional activity.
